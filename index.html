I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Cal Poly Pomona with a record of crossing disciplines — from lunar drill design at NASA JPL to hands-on BAS commissioning at Siemens to building a quantum-assisted security robot for the STARS program.

My foundation is hands-on. I've restored a 1974 VW Baja Bug from the ground up, installed two off-grid solar systems, and built engineering hardware that has been presented to NASA JPL, Northrop Grumman, congressional representatives, and U.S. Senate offices.

At Siemens I developed practical expertise in building automation systems — running and terminating low-voltage wiring, commissioning APOGEE field controllers, testing BACnet networks, and diagnosing faults in the field. That hands-on instinct combined with a strong analytical foundation is what I bring to every project.

I hold a DoD Interim Secret Security Clearance and I'm actively seeking engineering roles and research opportunities where I can apply mechanical design, systems thinking, and field-level problem solving to meaningful problems.

Future Research Goals

Space Agriculture & Extraterrestrial Habitats

Long-term research direction — not current work

Show

My long-term research goal is developing the engineering systems that allow humans to grow food on the Moon, Mars, and in deep space — designing controlled-environment habitats, soil processing systems, atmospheric controls, and water recovery loops that make sustainable off-world settlements possible.

My existing work connects to this direction: regolith sampling at NASA JPL relates to processing lunar and Martian soil; NDVI-based crop monitoring establishes precision agriculture sensing skills; and HVAC/BAS experience at Siemens maps onto controlled-environment agriculture and habitat life support systems.

Lunar Agriculture

Growing systems using processed lunar regolith, artificial lighting, and closed water loops to sustain plant life in pressurized habitats.

Mars Growing Systems

Pressurized greenhouse structures, soil remediation strategies, and crop selection suited to Martian gravity and radiation environments.

Deep Space Food Production

Compact, high-efficiency food production modules for long-duration missions — integrating hydroponics, aeroponics, and nutrient recycling.

ISRU for Agriculture

Using in-situ resource utilization to extract water and process raw lunar and Martian soil into viable growing media.
